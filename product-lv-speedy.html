What to check on a used Louis Vuitton Speedy

Size number, canvas, and Bandoulière strap

The number and the strap are the model

A Speedy is a structured barrel with short handles and a top zip. The number in the name is the size class: 20, 25, 30, and 35 are the ones shoppers compare. A 25 is a small city bag. A 30 is the common middle size, about a foot wide, though years are not identical, so read the listing’s measurements. A 35 is roomier. A 20, sometimes sold as a Bandoulière, is smaller again.

Bandoulière means a removable shoulder strap. Listings shorten it to “B” or “B30.” A Speedy 30 without a strap is not a Speedy Bandoulière 30. If the strap is missing, the ask should say so. A Nano Speedy is a different, much smaller crossbody. A Keepall is a travel duffel. Leave those out of a Speedy 30 comparison.

What to check when buying used

  • Shape. A Speedy should hold a barrel, not collapse at the ends. Soft, slumped ends often mean a tired structure.
  • Handles and the leather tabs where they meet the canvas. Cracks, dryness, and water stains belong in the condition note.
  • Honey-colored vachetta can be ordinary patina. Sticky, peeling coating on the canvas, especially along the base, is a different problem.
  • Zipper travel and the interior, including stains and odor. A light odor note is a fact about that bag. Do not ignore it because the price is lower.
  • On a Bandoulière, the strap leather, the hardware, and whether the strap is actually in the package.
  • Interior date code, usually in a pocket. Compare it with what the title claims.

Questions

Is a Speedy 30 the same bag as a Speedy Bandoulière 30?

No. A classic Speedy is a top-handle barrel without a shoulder strap. Bandoulière, often shortened to B, adds a removable strap. Compare 30 with 30 and strap with strap. A Nano Speedy is a much smaller crossbody and is not a Speedy 25.

What should I check on a used Louis Vuitton Speedy?

The barrel should hold its shape. Check the vachetta handles for cracks, the zipper, the base canvas for peeling, the interior date code, and any odor note. Dark honey-colored handles can be normal patina. Cracks and sticky coating are condition issues.

How do I compare Speedy prices across resale shops?

Match size number, canvas (Monogram, Damier Ebene, Damier Azur, or Epi), and whether the strap is included. Then read that shop’s condition grade and add shipping and tax. LuxArb has not saved a Speedy list price. Search buttons open the shops.
