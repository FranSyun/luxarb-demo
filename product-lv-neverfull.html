What to check on a used Neverfull MM

The Neverfull is an open tote in coated canvas with vachetta leather handles and side trim. PM is the small size, MM is the middle everyday size, and GM is the largest. This page is MM only, in Damier Ebene, the brown checker canvas. Monogram canvas and Damier Azur are different products. A Neverfull Neo uses a different handle construction. A Speedy is a zipped barrel, not this tote.

  • Canvas corners and the base. Rubbed corners are common. Peeling or sticky coating is a condition problem, not ordinary wear you should ignore.
  • Vachetta handles and trim. A honey color is normal patina. Cracks at the handle holes, water stains, and dryness should be written on the listing.
  • Interior. Many Damier Ebene Neverfulls have a red interior. Confirm the photos. Stains and odor matter. Rebag notes a light odor on item 445612/33.
  • Side-pocket zippers and the date code, often inside a pocket. Rebag prints brand code GI1152. Read the code the listing shows.
  • The pochette, the small removable pouch. If it is not in the live photos, it is not included. Do not add a pouch price in your head. Fashionphile item #1992692 is sold out and is not a current ask.

Questions

What is the difference between Neverfull PM, MM, and GM?

PM is the small tote, MM is the middle everyday size, and GM is the largest. This page is Damier Ebene Neverfull MM only. A Neverfull Neo has a different handle construction. A Speedy is a different bag.

Does darker vachetta mean the Neverfull is damaged?

Honey-colored handles and trim are ordinary patina on vachetta leather. Cracks, water stains, and a sticky or peeling canvas coating are condition problems. Read the photos of the handles, corners, and base.
