What to check on a used Hermès Kelly

Size, Sellier or Retourne, leather, and the lock set

LuxArb has not saved a checked list price for a Kelly. The buttons below open search. A Kelly is not the Birkin 30 on our comparison page. See the Disclaimer.

Kelly is not a Birkin

Both are Hermès, and the asks still do not travel together. A Kelly has one top handle, a trapezoid front, and a turn-lock that holds the flap with a strap, called a sangle. A Birkin has two handles and a different opening. If a shop title says only “Hermès black 28,” open the photo and name the silhouette before you compare it with anything.

Our checked Birkin page is a 30 in Togo Noir with palladium hardware, and even those two listings are not the same bag. Use that page for Birkin notes. Use this one to keep a Kelly search honest.

Size and construction

The number is the width in centimeters. The sizes people most often mix up are 25, 28, and 32. A 25 is a small top-handle. A 28 is the middle everyday size. A 32 is larger. A Kelly with a detachable shoulder strap is a different configuration from a top-handle-only bag, even when the centimeter matches.

Size 25, 28, and 32 are different bags. Do not average them.
Sellier or Retourne Sellier is rigid, with a flat front and an outside seam. Retourne is softer, with the seam inside.
Leather Epsom stays structured. Togo is pebbled and firmer than Clemence. Swift is smooth. Box calf shows scratches.
Color and hardware Noir is Hermès black. Gold hardware and palladium hardware are separate specs.
Strap A shoulder strap included, or not, is part of the listing. Do not assume it is in the photos.
Lock set Lock, keys, and clochette are a real part of the purchase. A missing set is not a small extra.

What to check when buying used

  • The flap sits square and the sangles are not cracked, stretched, or replaced with a different color.
  • The lock turns with the keys in the photos. If the listing says lock and keys are not included, price that bag, not a full set.
  • Handle shape. A dry, darkened, or cracked handle is condition, not “patina” you have to accept without reading the note.
  • Base and feet. Dragging wear on the bottom corners is common and should be in the photos.
  • The blind stamp the listing actually prints. The letter marks the year. Use the stamp on that bag. Do not copy a year from a different Hermès listing, including the stamp A note on our Birkin card.
  • Smell, color transfer, and any replaced hardware. Hermès descriptions are specific. A one-line grade is not enough.

LuxArb does not authenticate Hermès. Rely on the shop’s process, the interior stamp photos, and the return rules.

How to compare Kelly prices across shops

Pick one line and stay on it: Kelly 28 Sellier Epsom Noir with gold hardware and full lock set, or whatever you actually want. Search that line. Throw out anything that is a Birkin, a Kelly Danse, a Kelly Pochette, or a different centimeter. Condition words are each shop’s own. Add shipping, tax, and duties before you treat a lower ask as money saved.

Questions

Is a Hermès Kelly the same bag as a Birkin?

No. A Kelly is a single-handle trapezoid with a front turn-lock. A Birkin has two handles and a different flap. A Kelly 28 price is not a Birkin 30 price. LuxArb’s checked Birkin snapshot is a separate page.

What is the difference between Kelly Sellier and Retourne?

Sellier is the rigid construction with a flat front and the seam on the outside. Retourne is softer, with the seam turned inside. They are both Kelly bags, and they are not the same listing.

Which Kelly size should I compare?

Compare the centimeter in the name: 25, 28, or 32 are the sizes shoppers mix up most often. A shoulder strap, when the listing includes one, is a further difference. Read the listing’s own measurements.
