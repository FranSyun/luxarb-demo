LuxArb is a price-comparison site for pre-owned luxury, operated by Frank Syun. Please read this before using any price on the site.

1. Snapshots, not a live quote

Each card records a public asking price we could open on the check date printed on the card (timezone Asia/Shanghai). Those figures:

  • can change, sell through, or be withdrawn after we check them;
  • are not an offer from LuxArb and do not guarantee you can buy the item at that price;
  • are list prices only. If a button says “platform search — verify live price,” we did not capture one in-stock ask on that platform.

2. Condition, authenticity, and “the same item”

Two listings are rarely equivalent. A gap can disappear because of:

  • condition grades that do not share a scale (Excellent, Very Good, Great, and similar labels);
  • year, stamp or reference, dial, and accessories (dust bag, box, lock, keys, papers);
  • authentication and each platform’s return rules — counterfeit and description risk stays with the buyer and the selling platform;
  • color, wear, repair, and odor that a short card cannot fully show.

3. Shipping, fees, and taxes

A lower list price is not money saved. Costs that can close a gap include:

  • domestic or international shipping, insurance, and duties;
  • sales tax, payment fees, and any seller commission if you later resell;
  • return shipping inside the platform’s return window;
  • currency moves and the time your money is tied up.

A displayed list-price difference is not cash in hand. This site does not recommend buying on one platform to sell on another.

4. Links and commissions

“Shop on …” buttons open a public retailer listing or that platform’s search. They are not tagged partner links unless a program has been approved and the tagged URL is set in our configuration. When a real partner link is in use, the footer says we may earn a commission if you buy through it. Until then the footer says links may open retailer sites, and we may earn a commission when partner programs are active. A commission, if any, does not change the item’s list price.

5. Not financial or legal advice

Nothing on this site is investment, financial, or legal advice. Pre-owned markets move. A past gap does not predict a future result. Do your own research and follow the law and each platform’s terms.
