About LuxArb

LuxArb is a price-comparison site for pre-owned luxury. Each card names the platform, the list price, the condition grade when the listing shows one, and a direct link. We do not sell from our own stock.

How to compare pre-owned luxury prices

Match the model first. Then read condition, year, and fees. A lower list price is not money saved until those match.

STEP 01

Match the model

Align brand, size, material, color, and hardware or reference. A Birkin 30 is not a Birkin 25. A silver dial is not a white Roman dial.

STEP 02

Read the condition grade

Excellent, Very Good, and Great do not mean the same thing on every platform. Use the photos and that platform’s own definition.

STEP 03

Add fees and shipping

Shipping, tax, duties, and a return reserve can erase a small gap. The numbers on this site are list prices only.

STEP 04

Open the live listing

Shop buttons open the retailer page we checked, or that platform’s search when we did not capture a single listing. Confirm the price there.

What the prices mean

We record public asking prices for similar models. We do not adjust for shipping, tax, duties, or fees, and we do not claim two listings are the same physical item. Always verify on the platform.

Important: Prices below were read from public listings on 2026-10-29 (Asia/Shanghai). They can change or sell through. Condition, authentication, shipping, and fees change what you pay. This is not financial advice.
