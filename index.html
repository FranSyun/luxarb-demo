About LuxArb

LuxArb is a price-comparison demo for shoppers who want to see how similar pre-owned luxury bags and watches are listed across major resale platforms. We highlight sample comparisons so you can understand the idea — then check live prices, condition, and authentication on the platform itself before you buy.

How it works

A simple way to understand cross-platform prices. Real purchases still depend on condition, authenticity checks, shipping, and fees.

STEP 01

Find a listing

Browse pre-owned luxury on platforms such as The RealReal, Fashionphile, Rebag, Vestiaire Collective, and Chrono24.

STEP 02

Compare similar items

Look at nearby condition grades, year, and accessories on another platform to see how list prices differ.

STEP 03

Weigh the extras

Factor in shipping, taxes, platform fees, and authenticity policies before deciding where to buy.

STEP 04

Check the live price

Open the platform listing to confirm current price, stock, and seller details. Links may open partner sites.

Methodology note

Sample spreads compare list prices for similar brand, model, and approximate condition. They do not adjust for shipping, taxes, platform fees, accessory differences, or authentication outcomes. Always verify details on the live listing.

Important: All products below are fictional sample listings. Prices and spreads are for illustration only. Condition, authentication, shipping, and platform fees can change what a deal is really worth. This is not financial advice.
